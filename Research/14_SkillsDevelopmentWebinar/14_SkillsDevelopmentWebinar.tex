\textbf{Source:} 
Here are my notes from the skills development webinar I attended.
\begin{itemize}
	\item  Skill development is a crucial metric to be tracked throughout the EPQ program, it shows development of not just your project but the skills the EPQ program was designed to teach you
	\item  Key skill categories: Time management skills, Communication skills, Use of IT, Research skills
	\item  Lower and higher order skills are both important though these higher skills are more crucial in life
	\item  You must EVIDENCE your skills development over time, this is often through: the diary and log, prototypes / plans, your presentation, final evaluation, Artifact commentary and PPR
	\item  Especially important to thoroughly mention your thought process in detail throughout the diary
	\item  Cut your artifact production into steps and clearly log these
\end{itemize}
