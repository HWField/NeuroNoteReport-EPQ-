\section{Product Showcase Videos}
\label{research:ProductShowcaseVideos}
In order to produce a professional and engaging showcase video for my EP Artifact, I have been researching how other platforms present their products. This research has informed the script and structure of my own video as well as the style, tonality and most crucially the features i want to highlight.

to gain an understanding of how other platforms present their products I have watched a number of showcase videos from various platforms, including Notion, Evernote and OneNote. Below are my notes on each video and the key takeaways that I want to include in my own video.

\subsection*{Notion}
\textbf{Source:} \cite{notionWhatNotion2020}

\textbf{Notes:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Very calm tone
    \item Focuses on isolated features at a time
    \item visuals are focused on just the notion platforms
    \item frequently talks about customisability and flexibility
    \item uses adjectives like "powerful" and "simple" to describe the platform
    \item frequently uses "and much more" to imply that there are many more features than shown
    \item "grow and change with you", "as many pages as you need"
    \item "everything has its place"
    \item is never specific about a use case instead keeping it more vague
    \item "offers something \textit{new}"
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Evernote}
\textbf{Source:} \cite{**384Evernote}

\textbf{Notes:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item much more upbeat and fast pace music
    \item more visual representations of ideas rather than the Evernote platform itself
    \item has a Q and A style format with a problem and a solution
    \item question asker implies other platforms are clunky with their solutions whereas the answerer explains how Evernote is much simpler
    \item gives long lists when giving examples of features
    \item visuals align with music nicely 
    \item again emphasises customisability, using musical breaks and silence for emphasis
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{OneNote}
\textbf{Source:} \cite{VideoIntroducingOneNote}

\textbf{Notes:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item animations closely follow musical beats
    \item asks rhetorical questions to engage the viewer
    \item lots of synonyms for thoughts
    \item key selling point of multiple devices
    \item joygul feel throughout
\end{itemize}


From these three videos I have taken away a number of key points that I want to include in my own video:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Calm and focused tone
    \item High quality visuals and sound design
    \item Clear structure and narrative
    \item Highlighting key features in an engaging way
    \item Emphasising customisability and flexibility
    \item Using rhetorical questions to engage the viewer
    \item Using synonyms for key terms to keep the language fresh
    \item Using music to enhance the visuals and create a cohesive experience
\end{itemize}
