\section{Web Based Drawing}
\label{research:WebBasedDrawing}
I find the ability to draw and markup notes a crucial aspect of effective note-taking. The muscle memory gained through drawing helps me, and from further research many people as noted by this Peer Reviewed Scientific Study \cite{umejimaPaperNotebooksVs2021}. The same study found that the physical act of committing hand actions helps the recollection of information as physical space is used a memory medium (eg 1/3rd down the page as a technique to recall a certain value).

This solidifies my requirement that my note-taking artifact should enable the user to use handwritten methods to draw within a digital canvas. Next I looked to the technical feasibility of this.

One of my requirements is that my software is available on any of the users devices, this includes mobile devices such as iPads and other tablets. These devices notoriously are incompatible with many web technologies so I researched techniques to navigate this.

I came across \href{https://fabricjs.com}{Fabric JS} as a cross compatible JavaScript library that allows for the creation of highly customisable canvases supporting text, images and crucially touch input to produce written notes. After testing on my touch devices this seems like a feasible solution and I will explore further integrating with my findings
