\section{Writing vs Typing}
\label{research:WritingVsTyping}
\textbf{Source:\cite{muellerPenMightierKeyboard2014}}

This week I chose to handwrite my notes rather than type them, this was to explore the differences between the two mediums and how they affect my learning. I have found that typing is often faster and more efficient for me, but handwriting allows me to engage with the material on a deeper level. I will continue to explore this topic in future weeks.
