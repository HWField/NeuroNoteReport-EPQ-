\subsection{General}
\begin{enumerate}


    \item \href{https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/TTC-EC-CEA-AI-Report-12052022-1.pdf}{TTC-EC-CEA-AI-Report-12052022-1} - US government and EU report which highlights (in detail) many figures - needs verifying but likely to be accurate, contains lots references to further sources.
