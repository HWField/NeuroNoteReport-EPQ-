\section{Source Evaluation}
The evaluation of sources is a critical aspect of any research project, as it ensures the reliability and validity of the information used. In this section, I will evaluate the sources I have used throughout my research, focusing on their credibility, relevance, and potential biases to help assess their overall value to my project.

My evaluation will consider the following criteria:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Authority:} Who is the author or publisher? Are they recognized experts in the field?
    \item \textbf{Currency:} Is the information up-to-date? Does it reflect the latest research or developments?
    \item \textbf{Purpose:} What is the purpose of the source? Is it to inform, persuade, entertain, or sell?
    \item \textbf{Relevance:} Does the source directly relate to my research topic? Does it provide valuable insights or data?
    \item \textbf{Bias:} Does the source exhibit any bias? Are there any conflicts of interest that could affect the information presented?
    \item \textbf{Rating:} How would I rate the source based on the above criteria? This will be a subjective assessment based on my evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Notes:} Any additional comments or observations about the source that may be relevant to my research.
\end{itemize}

I also link each source to a Bibliography entry, which provides more detail on how to access the source and its full citation. This will help ensure that I can easily reference the sources in my final report and provide proper attribution.
