\section{Primary Research Interview and Analysis}
\label{research:PrimaryResearchInterviewAndAnalysis}
please find my notes below from an interview i conducted with an industry expert and degree holder in the field of organisational psychology. The interview was conducted on Thursday 11th January 2025. A recording of the interview was taken; i was requested to keep the audio private, however i have transcribed the key points below. The interview was conducted in person and lasted approximately 30 minutes.

Core Philosophy:

	•	“I live my life in systems”: The individual heavily relies on structured frameworks informed by organisational physiology and behavioural sciences.

Current Note-Taking Practices:

	•	Score for current approach: 8/10.

	•	Primary tools: Digital note-taking is central, leveraging tools like Plaude for voice input and AI-powered summarisation.
	
	•	Pros: Highly user-friendly interface and capabilities.

	•	Cost: Subscription at £150/year (or £79 annually when discounted).
	
	•	Usage frequency: A very frequent note-taker.

Concerns and Challenges:

	1.	Data Loss Risks:

	•	Issues with tools like HubSpot and OneNote highlight potential data loss.

	•	General mistrust of free tools due to security concerns.

	2.	Regulatory Impacts:

	•	Anticompetitive EU policies: Concern about the complexity and limitations of data migration between platforms.

Desired Features in a Note-Taking System:

	1.	Ease of Use:

	•	Cross-platform compatibility.

	•	Voice transcription with seamless conversion to text.

	•	Automatic action generation from notes.

	•	Ability to recognise speakers in meetings or discussions.

	•	An intuitive interface with a helpful wizard or guide for onboarding.

	2.	Offline Access:

	•	Essential for flexibility and reliability during connectivity issues.

	3.	Pricing and Value:

	•	Willing to pay up to £12/month, preferring lower subscription rates.

	•	Payment reinforces the perception of data protection and security.

Organizational Structure:

	•	Utilizes a 365-folder-and-page structure for organization and categorization of notes and tasks.

Additional Note:

The structure and features outlined reflect aspirations for a future-proofed note-taking system that integrates seamlessly into their workflow, ensuring data security, usability, and value for money.

\subsection*{My Takeaways:}

Speaking to an individual who has spent many years in the corporate sector focused on management and productivity has given me many insights surrounding crucial features I must include and some more aspirational targets

Throughout the interview data security and integrity was mentioned multiple times, whilst I am fully aware I cannot match the resources companies like Microsoft and Google will have access to I will ensure I make this fully transparent to any user who may test my software, this artifact is very much a proof of concept and will require investment in resources if it continues past that point

Many AI focused features such as auto transcription and note making were also mentioned which I would love to incorporate into my project however I am not sure how feasible this will be currently. Unfortunately I have quite a limited budget to spend on EPQ and cannot afford many API calls to professional services such as OpenAI models like GPT4

I will need to explore how I can ensure offline access is possible so that users can at the very least still view their information if they have a temperamental internet connection.

Should a program like mine become a crucial part of a users ecosystem though there is clearly a market looking to pay a high price for this functionality.
