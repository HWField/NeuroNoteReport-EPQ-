\section{Notetaking Annoyances}
\label{research:NotetakingAnnoyances}
This research is about documenting the issues, annoyances and potential improvements I find whilst note-taking In my day-to-day life.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{XX}
    \toprule

    \textbf{Issue} & \textbf{Takeaway}

    \\
    \midrule

    Linking to specific references is not possible within OneNote, I wanted to link a specific text field within a research to my homework page but this was not possible
                   &
    Ability to easily dynamically link specific elements or views of notes to another \\

    Tracking data modifications was not flexible enough, revisions and creation dates of specific aspects was not possible and I can't search my research based on the date written
                   &
    Creation data and modification dates of all elements should be recorded (possibly for one month but adjustable depending on storage space available)

    \\
    Repeating actions in OneNote to minimise mouse usage. For example wanting to change colour of many pieces of text after selecting them
                   &
    Create a universal repeat action key which repeats the last operation on the selected text

    \\

    Repetitive nature of certain notes, I find myself repeating records and tasks between multiple pages (such as writing about homework tasks both within my diary and the homework page)
                   &
    Another use case for stronger text linking tools

    \\



    Having to continually jump between notebooks/sections in OneNote and having to find the page I want each time

                   &
    Ability to have multiple notes opening for editing at once similar to a web browser


    \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
