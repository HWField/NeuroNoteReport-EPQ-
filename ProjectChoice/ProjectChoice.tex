These ideas were inspired by the various topics and themes I had previously explored in my studies and personal interests. At this point I was considering a wide range of ideas including: 

\begin{enumerate}
    \item A Dissertation on how political beliefs throughout history have influenced technological advancements
    \item A video essay on the history of human thought and how this has influenced modern society
    \item A deep dive on AI and Machine Learning, exploring the technical aspects of how these systems work and their implications on society
    \item A documentary exploring the digital divide in society and how this is impacting different communities
    \item A research project on the impact of digital platforms on the education system
    \item A Dissertation of human learning and interactivity with machines
\end{enumerate}

Finally (after much careful deliberation) I was able to narrow down my ideas and select a project to pursue.
A key deciscion making factor for me was the impact the EPQ could have on my interdisciplinary skills that would be most applicable to my future studies and career. I wanted to ensure that I was able to develop skills that would be useful in a variety of contexts, rather than just focusing on one specific area. Whilst the historically grounded research projects were very interesting, I felt that they would not provide me with the same level of skill development in areas such as computer science and 'cutting edge' research which I would gain from my ideas which targeted more modern technolodgies. For this reason I have chosen to pursue a project which explores the impact on our population that modern technologies have had, as well as their implications on society. I believe that this project will allow me to develop a wide range of skills that will be useful in my future studies and career, while also allowing me to explore a topic that I am passionate about. I am still very keen to learn from and explore historical contexts however, and so I am going to ensure I incorporate this into my project where possible to further enrich my understanding of the topic.
