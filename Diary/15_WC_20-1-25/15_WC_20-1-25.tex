\subsection*{Achievements, Thoughts and Ideas:}
Having collected a sufficient number of responses for my survey I have analysed each submission and made a list of takeaways to implement within my architecture: \nameref{research:SurveyAnalysis}

In Lessons this week we examined some prior EPQ projects and awarded them marks. This exercise has given me a much greater understanding of what marks are awarded for in the EPQ program. I now have a renewed focus to keep my diary and other supporting documentation updated as there is no use creating an amazing artifact should my journey and skill development not be recorded.

Building on last weeks generalised UI research I have continued my \nameref{research:SpecificUIResearch} by exploring my competitors offerings more closely, this has shown me a number of subtle quality of life features I can easily add that will help me differentiate from their products

Whilst conducting this research, I came across this Y Combinator forum page -\cite{BuildingRoamlikeNetworked} which included lots of information about much smaller project with similar philosophies to mine. Whilst browsing I saw many implementations of graphs and linked data and whilst I don't have time to document each and every one I can still see a gap for software which I believe my artifact can fill.

I also completed some research on \nameref{research:WebAccessibility} and learnt how to make my webapp more accessible for users with differing access requirements
