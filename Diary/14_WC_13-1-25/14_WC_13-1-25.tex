\subsection*{Achievements, Thoughts and Ideas:}
Although I haven't yet received my target number of form submissions I conducted an interview with an expert in my industry (Notes here: \nameref{research:PrimaryResearchInterviewAndAnalysis}), before conducting the interview I researched some effective techniques to maximise the opportunity to gain insight. 

Previously I had begun learning to use the industry standard UI design tool Figma, with this knowledge and the context of my project in mind I began researching and collating references to Ui's I found online, extracting the pros and cons from each as I began to think about my projects look and feel: \nameref{research:GeneralUIExploration}. Although I don't yet have a complete idea about my projects end design I have made good progress and now have a few ideas / references which I could implement.

Additionally I spent some time Researching (\nameref{research:UserManagementAndSecurity}) and implementing (\nameref{prototype:AuthenticationSystem}) a secure login system for my project. This enables me to confidently invite other users once I reach that stage, confident that they won't be able to access data they shouldn’t (at least to the same standards the rest of the industry uses). To test this in the future I have already reached out to some friends of mine both studying and working in the data security to conduct what is called "pen testing" where, with authorisation form the software owner, they attempt to breach security systems and find workaround which can be fixed

In lessons this week I created my skills audit and began exploring the techniques and knowledge I want to progress. To further explore this I created some notes on this \nameref{research:SkillsDevelopmentWebinar}
