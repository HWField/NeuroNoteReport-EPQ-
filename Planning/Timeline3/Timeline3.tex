\subsection*{Reflection on timeline 3}
Whilst no very significant changes were made to my timeline in this period, I did find that some tasks took longer than I had initially anticipated. For example, I had initially planned for the walkthrough video to only take around an hour or two to record and edit, however I found that as I wanted to make the video high quality and include a lot of detail the process ending up taking 2 days to complete. Additionally, I found that I had to spend more time than I had initially anticipated on testing and fixing bugs within my application, as I wanted to ensure the application was as polished as possible before releasing it to users. This time does seem to have paid off as I had very few bug reports from users however, and the application has been very well received.
