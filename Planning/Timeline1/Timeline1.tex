\subsection*{Reflection on timeline 1}
\textbf{Date: 7/3/25}

With regard to the development of my software I am currently two weeks ahead of my plan, thanks to careful testing and research I haven't had any major issues that have delayed my progress and it has been 'smooth sailing'. 

Although this could mean I can move all of my targets ahead by two weeks I believe I may begin to face unexpected delays in the latter implementation as I tie together the various aspects of my project. Therefore I am choosing to keep my timeline roughly the same, these goals are all fully achievable and the time I have gained now can be used as overhead should a different task take longer than expected.
